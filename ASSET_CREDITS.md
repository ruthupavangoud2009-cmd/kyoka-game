# KYOKA — Asset Credits & License Attribution

All assets, shaders, audio, models, and systems in KYOKA are 100% original, procedural, or derived from open-source free-tier components in accordance with the ₹0 zero-budget policy and legal licensing guidelines.

---

## 1. Procedural 3D Art & Models
- **KyokaCharacterModel**: 100% original procedural humanoid mesh generator created by KYOKA Studios. Built with bone hierarchies, custom vertex topologies, and procedural styling.
- **KyokaTextureGenerator**: 100% original procedural texture synthesis for cyber grids, 9-sliced panels, buttons, anime facial textures, and crosshairs.
- **KyokaMaterialFactory**: 100% original multi-tier shader and material pipeline utilizing Unity Universal Render Pipeline (URP) Lit and procedural unlit fallbacks.
- **KyokaWorldBootstrap**: 100% original procedural architecture, skyscrapers, branching trees, crystalline ores, and regional landmarks.

---

## 2. Procedural Audio Engine
- **KyokaSoundBank**: 100% original procedural synthesizer written in C# generating real-time PCM waveforms via `AudioClip.Create`:
  - Gunshots (Assault Rifle, Combat Shotgun, High-Caliber Sniper)
  - Cyber Katana slashes
  - Weapon reloads & mechanical clicks
  - Hitmarkers & Headshot chimes
  - Loot pickup chimes
  - Skydiving glider wind loops
  - BOOYAH! victory fanfare chord progressions
  - Atmospheric synthwave lobby and combat drum tracks
