# KYOKA Game Design

## Vision
Original hybrid realistic/anime open-world multiplayer game for PC, Android and iOS.

## Regions
- KYOKA City: urban exploration, NPCs, shops, properties and missions.
- Wildlands: nature, gathering and survival.
- Ocean Realm: islands and boat exploration.
- Anime Realm: original powers, quests and boss encounters.
- Battle Islands: separate match maps.
- Creative World: construction and custom experiences.

## Modes
Open world, battle royale, anime RPG, survival, creative and roleplay.

## Vehicles
Cars, motorcycles, boats and aircraft.

## Art direction
Realistic environmental materials and lighting, anime-inspired characters and stylized readable fictional ability effects.

## Production note
The included project is an early playable foundation. Large world art, production animation, full content, online backend, dedicated hosting and final platform certification remain future engineering/content work.
